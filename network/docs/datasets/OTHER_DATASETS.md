# Other datasets in the shared Drive folder

Read-only survey of the [`datasets` folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/18BhEPZwIDCP44HoxkpXShardOaw2r3Tr), 21 October 2026, using the USP-signed-in Drive browser session. This describes the ten top-level collections **other than** the three `sideseeing-hospital-*` folders already covered by the [hospital plan](README.md). I inspected folder listings and representative capture folders. I did not download archives, videos, notebooks, or CSV contents, so scientific purpose and completeness remain provisional where names alone do not establish them. No Drive or RustFS files were changed.

## Relationships to check before another migration

1. `citizen-audit-sidewalks-sp-2025` and the Brás branch of the pilot may duplicate subsets of `2025_Auditoria_Calcadas`. Compare paths, sizes, and hashes before assigning new RustFS keys.
2. `jbcs-2024`, `icra-2025`, and `cities-extras` include hospital-site names also seen in `sideseeing-hospital-*`. They may be selections, annotations, or additional captures; folder names alone do not prove equality.
3. The `cities`, `cities-extras`, `ugpn-2024`, and `prototypes` collections may share capture files or illustrate successive research stages. Keep their source identities separate until object-level matching and notebook review clarify provenance.

This survey is a content explanation, not a transfer manifest. File counts, total bytes, checksums, licenses, and participant/privacy conditions remain to be inventoried before any bulk copy or publication.
