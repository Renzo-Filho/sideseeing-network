## Rules carried over from the protocol

- One pass per sidewalk side; any repeated passage is logged as an overlap or repositioning, not new coverage.
- Crossings are recorded continuously through approach, waiting, traversal and arrival. A crossing merely seen from a sidewalk does not count.
- Neighboring routes should share short sections of the **same physical sidewalk** so sessions can be associated. The [solo protocol](PILOT_PROTOCOL.md) names O-PG and O-GR and gives field-selection rules; the drawing itself has not been annotated with them.
- If a passage is closed or differs from the drawing, record the deviation; do not invent a connection to match the plan.

## What the pilot should establish

1. Whether the moto g 5G (2023) and vest setup produce usable video, GPS and the intended inertial streams.
2. Whether the two sides of a narrow residential street (Carpenter, Miller) and the parallel mid-block passages remain distinguishable despite GPS error.
3. Whether crossings appear continuously in the capture.
4. Whether separate sessions can be associated through overlaps.
5. Actual field time per route, repositioning burden, storage, battery use, transfer time and first review effort, as a measured basis for later full-area estimates.

## Session workflow

No time allowance is proposed until one route has been walked. Run one route first as the measured trial, including setup, quality check and transfer.

| Stage | Output |
|---|---|
| Reconnaissance and setup | Verify passages are public and non-UIC, crossings are usable, and the vest view is stable |
| Capture | One identifiable session per route |
| Immediate quality check | Verify expected streams, duration, crossings and turns are visible; list targeted retakes |
| Contingency | Crossing waits, device adjustments, interruptions |

## Manifest and first analysis

For each session, record:

- route color/ID and original capture folder;
- collector/device ID, app and acquisition settings, and vest/mount setup;
- actual time, start/end and direction;
- passages and crossings completed;
- overlaps and repositioning;
- interruptions, deviations and quality status.

Preserve the original files. Test this phone's actual export before interpreting its sensor streams.

Use a documented Cook County aerial source for the first overlay. The [Cook County imagery service](https://gis.cookcountyil.gov/imagery/rest/services/Basemap/CookImagery/ImageServer) and [2025 orthophoto service](https://img.cookcountyil.gov/imagery/rest/services/CookOrtho2025/ImageServer) are candidates. Record imagery year, retrieval date and spatial reference. Plot raw timestamp-ordered GPS points per route, inspect video around crossings, and log observation gaps separately from positioning disagreement.

## Pilot completion and open items

The pilot is complete when every drawn passage has a documented outcome, raw sessions and provenance are preserved, and findings report recording quality, actual effort and protocol changes. An unusable passage is a finding, not successful coverage.

Open before fieldwork:

- Mark the actual start/end landmarks and the two short overlap sections on a working map copy, using the rules in [PILOT_PROTOCOL.md](PILOT_PROTOCOL.md).
- Check current access to the drawn mid-block paths and crossing arms.
- Record the date and confirm the moto g 5G (2023), MultiSensor and vest setup with a test export.
