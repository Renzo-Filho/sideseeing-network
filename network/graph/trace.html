Morgan reference check

Cook County 2025 orthophoto (about 15 cm per pixel), Morgan St from Tilden to Grand, both sides. Nothing from the recordings is shown; please do this before looking at any model output.

What to do
  1. Check the city's curbs (2010). Zoom to a block; if every curb line there sits on today's curb, press All in view match. Click a line to cycle it: matches, changed, unchecked. Where one changed (a new corner, a bump-out), draw today's curb with Curb line (correction).
  2. Trace the back of the sidewalk on each block face: where the paving ends on the property side (building face, fence, planting), from corner to corner. Where it steps, follow it.
  3. Paving edge, road side: only where a grass or planting strip separates the paving from the curb.
  4. Mark a line unsure when trees, shadows or vehicles hide it; leave out what you cannot see.

Draw: click to add points, double-click to finish, Esc to stop. Edit moves vertices of your lines; Delete removes one by clicking it.
