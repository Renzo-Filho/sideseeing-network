# What São Paulo and Chicago's urban data can and cannot measure

**Evidence cutoff: 22 September 2026.** This is a methodological survey of project documentation, source acquisition reports, machine-readable validation receipts and bounded experiments. It asks a basic urban-science question: when similarly named data fields are put side by side, do they describe the same urban quantity? The answer is frequently no. See the [experiment ledger](EXPERIMENT_LEDGER.md) for scripts and precise audit trails. Percentages below refer to the specified units and denominators; none is a citywide error rate unless explicitly stated.

## 1. Study design and present boundary

The comparison cohort is **96 São Paulo municipal districts and 77 Chicago Community Areas**. The original comprehensive design contains 13 families: street density (M1), junctions (M2), block size (M3), block shape (M4), street hierarchy (M6), cadastral entities (M7), footprint coverage (B1), reported floors (B2), constructed floor area (B3), land-use diversity (U1), workplace jobs (U2), residents (U3), and bus supply (U4). A six-family M1/M6/B1/BV/U3/U4 alternative has been reopened for discussion. BV is a new grid-derived vertical-form family, not an alias for B2 or B3. The [status](../MODEL_STATUS.md) and [gate ledger](../chicago/CHICAGO_13_FAMILY_COMPLETION_LEDGER.md) explain why no common fit or ranking exists. A sealed 173-unit candidate table contains **6,209 long-form rows and 37 candidate/diagnostic columns**; those are construction outputs, not 37 validated feature coordinates.

The three recurring failure modes are **different observation universes** (tax accounts versus physical parcels; formal jobs versus covered jobs), **different spatial support** (whole fiscal entities versus district-clipped land; source cells versus partial cells), and **different meanings for null or other labels** (no lot, unknown use, nonparcel area, unmapped road, missing height). These mechanisms must be reported separately. A complete numeric matrix does not remove them.

## 3. Highest-value data discoveries

### 3.1 Fiscal geometry, cadastral entities and constructed area

São Paulo's entity key required a substantive correction: all **39,521 nonzero-condominium parcel geometry rows** use lot code `0000`, so sector–block–lot conflated distinct condominiums. The accepted preparation uses sector–block–condominium for these cases and sector–block–lot otherwise. Two of 39,519 condominium entity keys have multiple geometries; 630 competing links remain excluded. This correction lifted located constructed-area mass from an **intermediate erroneous 61.6%** to **97.3032%** under the declared matching rule. The earlier value is historical, not the result. The source retains **3,920,972 unique fiscal accounts** with 597,424,198 m² of fiscal constructed area; 3,842,434 accounts and 581,313,100 m² meet the accepted location/aggregation rule. This is a fiscal proxy, not independently surveyed built floor area. [SP handoff](../sp/SP_DATA_RESOLUTION_HANDOFF.md).

São Paulo prepared **3,145,436 municipal Overture buildings**. **91.95%** have some positive parcel overlap, but Grajaú's overlap is only **57.75%**, compared with roughly **99.5%** in Brás and Itaim Bibi. A building–parcel intersection is only a candidate link. Thus the high fraction of *IPTU area located* does not prove that IPTU covers all physical buildings, especially in peripheral districts. Treat this as a testable source-selection concern, not proof of a specific informal-settlement bias.

Chicago's Cook residential source has positive area in **439,693/439,730** Chicago-filtered records, yet **32,525** stories are top-coded `3 Story +`, **8,950** are split-level, and 11 are missing. Condo building area is populated on only **73,251/287,189 unit records**, may repeat by parent, and is not distinct-building area coverage. In the 2024 commercial slice, `stories` and `gross_building_area` have **zero** populated records; 29,976/32,930 rows have positive `bldgsf`, 5,983 have net rentable area. DuPage's scoped parcel layer has no floors or constructed-area fields. Chicago benchmarking is a limited large-property/campus source, not an all-stock census. These are structural and semantic B2/B3 gaps; a source search may help, but current fields cannot be summed into an equivalent all-stock area. [Chicago acquisition](../../analysis/results/Chicago/chicago_cadastral_2026_09_18/README.md), [entity decision brief](../chicago/ENTITY_SOURCE_DECISION_BRIEF.md).

### 3.2 U1: the area that is missing is different in each city

The original SP U1 is **seven-class entropy of cadastral-entity counts**, with an area sensitivity. Chicago's local U1 is **eight-class entropy of CMAP primary land-use polygon area**. Whole SP entity assignment and Chicago district clipping also differ. A common area ontology therefore requires recomputation in **both** cities; merely renaming categories cannot harmonize the existing values. CMAP primary and secondary uses are separate fields; counting `LANDUSE2` as another full polygon would double count area. [CMAP multiple-use guidance](https://cmap-repos.github.io/LUI-wiki/CodingMultipleUses.html).

The bounded exact-area experiment sampled **Brás, Alto de Pinheiros, São Domingos, Loop, Albany Park and Humboldt Park**, chosen as central, residential and industrial contrasts. It reads selected/adjacent SP partitions and spatially bounded CMAP data, clips/unionizes by category, and uses each district's prepared **land area** as the common *denominator concept*. These six values are exact for the source polygons read within the bounded partition strategy, **not** a random estimate of citywide coverage. Neighbor files added 0.47 percentage points of Brás land and 0.98 of São Domingos in raw lot coverage; farther-file edge effects are unmeasured. [Exact-area report](../../analysis/results/SP_CHI/u1_developed_area_2026_09_22/README.md).

| Six mapped-use classes | 57.9% | 55.6% | 63.1% | 42.3% | 66.2% | 72.3% |
| Four occupied classes | 54.6% | 50.4% | 50.1% | 34.3% | 64.1% | 58.9% |

**Interpretation:** SP's no-lot fraction is outside the fiscal geometry universe in the selected/adjacent files, not a null use field. Chicago `6000` is a mapped class for nonparcel/unclassifiable support, not absent polygon coverage. The Loop's **24.7% observed open space** is yet another category, not missing data. Calling all three residuals “unknown land use” would erase the very phenomenon worth studying. We have not independently certified that every 2023 `6000` polygon is a street or that every SP no-lot polygon is road/right-of-way.

In the three SP units, no-lot land inside mapped ordinary `Quadra` accounts for **8.5%, 7.2%, 7.8% of district land**; outside mapped block polygons it accounts for **14.9%, 15.0%, 11.7%**. Of 16 earlier points without an accepted SP use, 15 were outside raw lot and one was inside raw lot without accepted use. Of those 15 no-lot points, ten fall outside mapped blocks, four in ordinary blocks, one in a plaza/median block plus mapped park. These are **contextual leads**, not independently labeled true land uses. The no-lot area inside ordinary blocks warrants targeted parcel/source review; the outside-block area calls for right-of-way/public-space mapping. Neither can be reclassified as residential or commercial by inference alone.

The `mixed/other` SP label also hides heterogeneous raw labels. A strict sensitivity that recodes only explicit and unanimous fiscal “predominantly residential/commercial” descriptions recovers **7.3, 0.9, and 3.8 percentage points of district land** in Brás, Alto and São Domingos; **7.2%, 10.6%, 1.9%** remains mixed, respectively. This does not allocate the actual area of each use within a parcel. Chicago primary `1216` mixed commercial/residential occupies **0.8%, 2.9%, 1.6%** of the respective Chicago district land; CMAP's primary/secondary rule is not equivalent to SP fiscal predominance. [Dominance sensitivity](../../analysis/results/SP_CHI/u1_developed_area_2026_09_22/sp_dominant_use_sensitivity.csv).

Category choice changes substantive interpretation. Brás/Loop conditional normalized entropy is **0.706/0.735** for six mapped classes but **0.774/0.662** for four occupied classes: the order reverses. These entropies exclude residual mass from their normalization. Calling either a *whole-district* diversity measure without reporting support would be misleading. All-land entropy is weaker with current data: the SP parks layer is partial; zoning is planned use, while [GeoSampa predominant-use maps](https://metadados.geosampa.prefeitura.sp.gov.br/geonetwork/srv/resources/datasets/73e0158b-2ec0-485c-b609-300f604afae7) derive from IPTU and cannot independently repair fiscal noncoverage. [MapBiomas land cover](https://brasil.mapbiomas.org/iniciativas-e-produtos/cobertura-e-uso-da-terra/cobertura-10m/cobertura/) can contextualize built/vegetated cover but cannot label residential versus commercial function.

A subsequent [six 400 m OSM tile pilot](../../analysis/results/SP_CHI/u1_osm_tiles_2026_09_23/README.md) deliberately centered on saved U1 diagnostic points found `landuse=*` polygon coverage of **2.5–6.5%** in three SP tiles and **68.2–99.8%** in three Chicago tiles. Two SP no-lot/inside-block centers remained unlabeled; broad OSM residential polygons covered two Chicago `6000` centers, which could overgeneralize use over nonparcel support. This is evidence about **sampled map completeness and polygon semantics**, not citywide OSM completeness or ground-truth accuracy. It weakens the proposal to use unaugmented OSM polygons as the common area-use source. The OSM API XML and hashes are locally retained; a live query does not reproduce an immutable historical snapshot.

The follow-up [independent case audit](../../analysis/results/SP_CHI/u1_independent_cases_2026_09_23/README.md) inspected seven fixed coordinates in aerial imagery. A Brás no-lot point **inside a mapped ordinary block** visibly falls on rail tracks; another SP no-lot point falls on a road. Conversely, two Chicago CMAP `6000` points lie on an alley and street edge even though broad OSM `residential` polygons cover them. This demonstrates that *inside-block no-lot* is not synonymous with a missing taxable land use, and high OSM tagged area is not parcel-use accuracy. The evidence is case-level; SP image capture dates are unverified and two paved/roof-adjacent cases remain ambiguous. No residual-area proportions were estimated.

### 3.3 POI diversity: promising alternate characteristic, unequal measurement process

A fixed 12-category Overture Places candidate measures **diversity of mapped destinations**. It does not measure land-use area or residential share. In the six selected units, exact polygon filtering retained 10,479 SP and 19,503 Chicago places. Brás has **5,443** and Loop **16,569** records. In a focused source audit, no taxonomy hierarchy appears on **254/5,443 (4.7%)** Brás records and **3,773/16,569 (22.8%)** Loop records. This “no taxonomy” statistic is distinct from the slightly larger “not in fixed 12 categories” residual in the first sample. Among no-taxonomy Loop records, **2,908** have confidence below 0.5. The base-provider mix differs sharply: Meta provides **96.6%** of Brás and **54.1%** of Loop places. Meta-only classified entropy still differs (0.537 versus 0.737), but Meta's fixed-12 coverage is **95.4%** in Brás and **66.6%** in Loop. A universal confidence threshold or Meta-only filter therefore does not establish comparable completeness. Publisher documentation describes duplicates, junk and incomplete fields, and says confidence is not calibrated across providers. [Six-unit sample](../../analysis/results/SP_CHI/u1_source_samples_2026_09_22/README.md), [two-anchor provider audit](../../analysis/results/SP_CHI/u1_developed_area_2026_09_22/README.md), [Overture Places guide](https://docs.overturemaps.org/guides/places/).

The same OSM tile pilot showed why a POI tag rule must exclude infrastructure: **159** raw Loop OSM amenity/shop/office/craft/tourism objects became **49** provisional venue candidates after removing benches, parking infrastructure and similar objects. Candidate counts across the six tiles were **45/0/0** in SP and **49/2/2** in Chicago, while pinned Overture records on identical tiles numbered **280/17/50** and **1,135/10/11**. Narrow exact-name/≤30 m matches were 7 in Brás, 18 in the Loop and 1 in Humboldt Park. These differences do not give precision/recall because the inventories have distinct definitions and no independent truth. The official [Chicago active-business-license view](https://data.cityofchicago.org/Community-Economic-Development/Business-Licenses-Current-Active/uupf-x98q) is a license universe with multiple records/keys, not a general POI inventory; a bounded official catalog search did not verify a dataset titled “Citywide POIs.” [Pilot report](../../analysis/results/SP_CHI/u1_osm_tiles_2026_09_23/README.md).

A seven-record [named-venue audit](../../analysis/results/SP_CHI/u1_independent_cases_2026_09_23/README.md) also revealed different error axes. Two malls' mapped points conflicted with operator/merchant address-number ranges by conservative lower bounds of **265–384 m**, including one OSM+Overture matched record. A care home missing from the OSM tile had a plausible Overture location, while an OSM-only Do-Rite record was supported by the operator and a Chicago license. The Ledge, an operator-described attraction, was Overture-coded `travel_service` at **0.942 confidence**. A low-confidence garbled Overture name remained unverified, not disproven. This fixed but purposive, single-reviewer sample supports *failure-mode discovery*, not precision/recall, category error rates or a cross-city correction factor.

The candidate name `destination_diversity_12` should remain **unweighted and outside the accepted model** until sampled listings are independently checked for existence, location and category, provider/duplicate sensitivities are predeclared, and added information beyond U1/U2/U3/B1 is demonstrated. The six selected districts and 20 saved listings per district do not support a citywide accuracy claim.

### 3.4 Streets and blocks: geometry is not physical topology

Matched Overture M1/M6 uses ten selected mapped-street classes. It measures mapped line length and class composition regardless of legal access. `unclassified` means a known low-order class, while `unknown` is undetermined; conflating them would hide source uncertainty. A full 173-unit audit identified edge cases: O'Hare's municipal code 99 includes **141.2 km** of local airfield linework, only about **20.7%** within 15 m of eligible Overture streets, and **83.0 km** without any raw Overture road within 15 m. In rural Marsilac, local `LOCAL` length is **209.8 km**, including 40.2 km near Overture excluded `track` and 45.4 km beyond 15 m of any raw Overture road. These combine policy, classification and mapping coverage; no universal correction factor follows. The 15 m comparison is an approximate length-weighted positional diagnostic, not an accuracy threshold. [M1/M6 review](../../analysis/results/SP_CHI/harmonization_2026_09_22_full_scope_review/m1_m6_review/README.md).

M2 source connectors can split one physical junction, while proximity can merge vertically separate roads. A **2.49 m** Loop connector pair is known from source levels to be a false distance merge; a **8.28 m** Brás pair is source-linked but lacks an independently certified physical-arm count. M3/M4 road polygonization creates slivers and misses non-road barriers; grade filtering removed some real reference support. Chicago Census blocks and São Paulo Quadra are useful *local* checks, not interchangeable gold standards. In a rail-centerline experiment at CHI:28, candidates increased **765→1,404**, polygons under 6 m width **5→359**, and candidate-to-reference IoU≥0.50 agreement **0.634→0.350** while reference coverage barely changed **0.534→0.542**. Rail centerlines were rejected as direct block boundaries; arbitrary 10–30 m corridors did not fix the method. A shoreline plus land-only sensitivity modestly improved Loop reference coverage **0.397→0.452**, but remains unaccepted because water masks and physical edge definitions differ. Chicago's CMAP 1511 rail right-of-way polygons and SP's 12 “influence/domain” polygons are not established as equivalent boundaries. [M2 review](../../analysis/results/SP_CHI/harmonization_2026_09_22_full_scope_review/junctions_v2/README.md), [reference fixtures](../../analysis/results/SP_CHI/harmonization_2026_09_22_full_scope_review/blocks_v2/reference_fixtures/README.md), [barrier tests](../../analysis/results/SP_CHI/harmonization_2026_09_22_full_scope_review/blocks_v3_barriers/README.md), [local rail test](../../analysis/results/SP_CHI/harmonization_2026_09_22_full_scope_review/blocks_v4_local_rail/README.md).

### 3.5 Buildings and vertical form: height cannot substitute for floors or floor area

The Microsoft/Overture eight-unit B1 pilot used the **same exact footprint union and land denominator**. Microsoft minus Overture is **−36.12 percentage points in Brás**, **−0.04** in Grajaú, **+8.64** in Itaim Bibi, and between **−0.87 and −2.28** in five Chicago fixtures. These differences are neither detection error rates nor proof of one source's accuracy. Microsoft appears upstream in Overture building lineage for both cities. Every selected SP Microsoft tile record has missing height (`-1`); Chicago height presence does not make paired height possible. GHSL's 2018 height and 2020 volume are gridded remote-sensing estimates. Their support, year and physical meaning differ from reported stories and fiscal floor area. The historical GHSL extract retains whole native boundary cells; final aggregation must fractionally allocate support and distinguish zero from NoData. In the paired run, actual AGBH raster headers use −1 NoData although a saved technical PDF lists 255; per-file masks governed processing. A city-extract boundary mismatch left about 0.009 m² of North Park support masked until one cell in each of three products was recovered from original ZIPs, with source alignment and value checks. Fourteen reprojected working geometries needed logged repair; the largest area change was about 236.65 m². These are recoverable processing/support issues, not evidence that the raster itself measured floor count. [Microsoft pilot](../../analysis/results/SP_CHI/microsoft_footprints_2026_09_22_review/README.md), [GHSL acquisition](../../analysis/results/SP_CHI/ghsl_public_2026_09_21/README.md), [paired candidate checks](../../analysis/results/SP_CHI/harmonization_2026_09_22_h1_h3/README.md).

### 3.6 Jobs, residents, transit and time

São Paulo RAIS 2022 contains **5,387,474 formal job links** in 29,594 CEP rows; the SP allocation experiments leave **508,844 geographically unlocated** under the documented policy. Chicago LODES WAC 2022 covers a different employment universe and uses Census block geography. The Chicago business-support sensitivity allocates **1,408,280.585** jobs inside Chicago and retains **49,982.415** outside from 1,458,263 jobs in whole intersecting positive-job blocks; a broader business-plus-transport support changes those masses modestly. **5,817** business-core blocks fall back to gross-area allocation. Its tiny geometric partition residual was handled under an explicit bound, with a maximum observed correction of 0.001215 m², not by dropping job mass. Both scenarios conserve mass, but neither proves RAIS–LODES comparability or exact establishment locations. [SP methods](../sp/SP_METHOD_DECISIONS.md), [Chicago U2 sensitivity](../../analysis/results/Chicago/chi_employment_sensitivity_2026_09_22_v2/README.md).

U3 pairs SP Census **2022** with Chicago Census **2020**; a complete table cannot turn these into a same-year population census. Chicago's earlier ACS 2023-labeled aggregate CSV had unresolved period/MOE/aggregation provenance; the later Census-block functional extension supersedes it for the paired candidate. U4's original SP v2 transit calculation included **nine metro and seven rail routes**; the paired companion correctly filters `route_type=3` for bus, lowering Brás weekday/400 m supply by about **16.6%**. SP expected-frequency service and CTA scheduled service still differ in calendar semantics, including holiday exceptions and a one-week scenario date gap. Source vintage, operator scope, service period and catchment geometry need explicit sensitivity before a common U4 meaning is accepted. [Paired construction](../../analysis/results/SP_CHI/harmonization_2026_09_22_h1_h3/README.md), [Chicago functional release](../../analysis/results/Chicago/chi_functional_2026_09_16_v2/README.md).

### 3.7 Incorrect or deceptively reassuring labels in the research pipeline

The source problem also appears in *our own* derived labels. The original SP model's PCA loading table attached **15 of 23 loadings to the wrong feature names**, because the table used scalar-first column order while the fitted embedding placed M6 earlier. Distances and ranks were numerically correct; interpretation of PCA axes was not. SP v2 corrected labels to the fitted embedding order, fixed input/weight/row alignment checks and saved fitted state. This is a strong methodological example: a validated distance matrix does not validate every explanatory label. The historical [failed audit](../archive/audits/SP_MODEL_VALIDATION.md) and [v2 correction](../sp/SP_MODEL_FIXES.md) should be cited together. The original v1 audit had **17 passing and 8 failing checks**; v2 reports **26 independent checks and 30 tests passing**, but source-equivalence problems remain.

A second labeling trap is São Paulo's Overture-derived column gross_floor_area_m2: its formula is footprint area times reported floors, with **one floor substituted when no floor count is reported**. In the 7,278,768-record metropolitan bounding-box extract, only **21,250 (0.2919%)** have positive floor counts; **7,257,518 (99.7081%)** take the one-floor fallback. This is a defined geometric scenario, not observed GFA, and it is not the SP fiscal B3 input. Height is present on 2,090,405 (28.7192%) records, still not an exact floor report. The bounding-box counts are not the 96-district stock. [Building pipeline](../sp/BUILDING_PIPELINE.md).

Chicago's local building dictionary clarifies that no_stories is **below-ground** stories, z_coord is not a height measure, and an unmaintained building-square-foot field is not reliable B3. The supplied morphological_data.csv contains a **dummy all-zero Brás row with ca=99**, explicitly excluded from Chicago processing; those zeros are not observed morphology. Chicago's local population CSV was labeled 2023 but lacked verified source period, margins of error and aggregation method; the later Census 2020 block source is the paired U3 basis. A DuPage catalog description names PROPCLASS, while its inspected live API uses REA017_PROP_CLASS/GIS_Prop_Class. These examples show why field names, filename dates and schema presence require record-level and publisher-definition checks. [Chicago execution](../chicago/CHICAGO_EXECUTION_REPORT.md), [discovery register](../chicago/CHICAGO_DATA_DISCOVERY_2026_09_18.md).

## 4. Implications for a paper and next evidence

A defensible paper would study **measurement transportability** rather than announce a cross-city urban similarity ranking. Candidate contributions include: (1) a typology of source-universe, class-label and spatial-support missingness; (2) how a superficially shared entropy reverses a neighborhood comparison when its ontology changes; (3) how topology from map lines diverges from physical street/block identity; and (4) why combining open sources with tax records can create double counting or false precision. These are **research questions**, not proven general laws. Six purposive U1 units, eight morphology pilots and eight B1 fixtures cannot estimate error rates over both cities.

The next small study should independently label predeclared stratified cases: SP no-lot inside/outside Quadra, SP raw-lot/no-use and mixed labels; Chicago `6000`, mixed primary/secondary use; paired POIs; divided/stacked junctions and block barriers; Microsoft/Overture footprint disagreements. Save reference source, reviewer, date, decision and uncertainty for each case. Then freeze the estimand, ontology, residual categories, decision thresholds and validation metrics **before** examining model rankings. Preserve all no-match and ambiguous mass. The [reproduction protocol](REPRODUCIBILITY.md) details the bounded design.
