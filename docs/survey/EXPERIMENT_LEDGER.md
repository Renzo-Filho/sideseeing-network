## Negative results that must survive later summaries

1. The six-class U1 screen's similar Brás/Loop entropy values concealed unequal omitted source mass; the exact four-class choice **reverses** their conditional entropy order.
2. Source polygon coverage and source *classification* coverage are different. Chicago `6000` is mapped, while SP no-lot support is outside read fiscal geometry.
3. Arbitrary road grade filters, rail centerlines and buffered rail corridors did not yield an accepted shared physical-block network. Narrow-polygon deletion would manufacture agreement.
4. Microsoft height cannot form a paired feature in the selected SP tiles; footprint differences are not a ground-truth contest.
5. Commercial schema fields called `stories` or `gross_building_area` had **zero populated values** in the tested 2024 slice; workbook text extracts did not restore them.
6. An all-pass numerical validation report and a complete raw matrix do not override open entity, source-universe, topology and ontology gates.
