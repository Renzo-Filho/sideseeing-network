# First visual triage of stitched Chicago faces — 28 September 2026

The [linkage script](../../../../scripts/link_chicago_m3_frozen_controls_2026_09_28.py) verifies the [frozen reference](../../chicago_m3_reference_zones_v2_2026_09_27/visual_reference_v2.json) hash, converts its pixel controls to EPSG:26916 and links them to the final 23,204-face geometry. The [linkage table](core_face_linkage.csv) and six orthophoto [overlays](CHI_49_R2_overlay.jpg) are reproducible review aids. The source geometry and frozen reference files were not changed.

The [first-pass triage](first_pass_triage.csv) records 12 specific cases. Nine previously unadjudicated CHI:57/CHI:63 faces look like ordinary street-bounded land in the imagery, so the earlier incomplete positive inventory cannot be used to estimate false-positive rate. Two CHI:49 points labeled rail-edge fall inside built residential land west of the visible railway. A local municipal-centerline check finds **S Stewart Avenue** about 10 m east of both face boundaries and **S Eggleston Avenue** to their west; the rail labels appear assigned to the wrong faces. Public road status and full property-side edges still need confirmation. The three CHI:76 negative points fall within one 21.18 km² global face whose *image core* is airport operating land. Since that face extends far beyond the 400 m core, no whole-face exclusion is authorized from these points.

The stitched geometry changes the inventory from the older window-clipped pilot: CHI:57 has seven global face representatives in the core versus eight clipped pilot candidates. CHI:76's giant face has its representative outside the core but covers all three controls; a representative-point-only join would silently miss it. The linkage script therefore includes faces that cover any frozen control even when their representative point lies outside the core.

This pass is **visual triage, not independent validation**. Candidate outlines were visible during inspection; there was one reviewer, no complete property-side boundary tracing, no independent road-status adjudication and no second review. The frozen CHI:57 B1 outline already has a documented street-status problem. The `visual_review_status` column in the linkage table remains `unreviewed` for formal block acceptance. No candidate was accepted, deleted, repaired or sent to M3/M4.

Next: confirm the two CHI:49 road statuses and correct the control placement in a separately versioned reference; make a candidate-blind full census with irregular, closed property-side boundaries and street-status records in a new zone set; partition the airport face using an operating-surface source; adjudicate named street gaps and seam cases; then test frozen repair rules on held-out zones. For M4, use exact outer perimeter and account for holes separately, and compare measured shapes against those new boundaries rather than the old four-corner sketches.
