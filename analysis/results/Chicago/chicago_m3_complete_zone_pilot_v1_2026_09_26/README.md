# Chicago M3/M4 complete-zone and motorway iteration, 26 September 2026

**Decision:** No citywide physical-block M3/M4 release. A useful ordinary-street construction recipe emerged, but the motorway inventory, missing named streets, rough-reference errors, and unverified shape boundaries prevent a reliable 77-area block layer. The published outputs here are diagnostic, not model features.

## Reproducible sequence

1. Four zones in CHI:11, CHI:32, CHI:49, and CHI:57 were chosen from Community Area interiors before inspecting candidate output. Cook 2025 orthophotos were acquired and 19 rough block outlines drawn. The image reference was frozen with SHA-256 before scoring. The post-overlay [adjudication](reference_adjudication_v1.json) excludes both CHI:57 outlines: one used an apparent internal drive as a boundary; the other crossed named S Komensky Avenue. The 17 remaining sketches cover ordinary residential and Loop contexts, not a representative Chicago sample. They are single-analyst imagery sketches, not property surveys.
2. `momepy.enclosures` on municipal centerlines, raw Cook ROW land faces, ROW with mapped alley reopening, and ROW plus ordinary Cook Road Edge were matched one-to-one against the frozen sketches at IoU ≥ 0.5. The 450 m construction halo excludes open tile-edge faces; candidate counts refer to the declared small core. In the two development cores with valid sketches, a 1 m alley opening left three Loop splits. The [sweep](alley_repair_sweep_summary.csv) found 3–4 m removed these splits, while 5 m merged a CHI:11 block and 6 m merged Loop blocks. The [3 m rule](../../../config/chicago_m3_alley_repair_v2.json) was frozen before the CHI:49 holdout was scored.
3. On the eight CHI:49 holdout sketches, municipal enclosures matched 8/8; raw ROW 1/8; reopened ROW alone 2/8; ROW plus Road Edge with 3 m alley opening 8/8. On all 17 usable sketches, municipal enclosures and the 3 m ROW-plus-edge version each matched 17/17. Median IoU was about 0.753 versus 0.863 respectively, and median absolute log area error about 0.262 versus 0.047. These are conditional outline diagnostics on selected, mostly rectangular blocks; they are not citywide recall, precision, or surveyed area accuracy. The full per-case and method tables are `development_*` and `holdout_*` CSV/JSON files.
4. The prior four CHI:49 motorway stress tiles were rerun under the 3 m rule: 35 candidates, 22 motorway flags, five of five clear positive sketches matched and unflagged, and two of two negative controls inside candidates flagged. These selected points did not constitute a complete non-block inventory.
5. A **new motorway-centered CHI:11 zone** was then selected before viewing candidate geometry. Its orthophoto reference was frozen separately. ROW plus Road Edge and 3 m alley opening yielded 18 core candidates for five rough positive sketches; only three crossed IoU 0.5. Of the 18, 13 were tiny (23–730 m²), fully exposed to the 40 m Overture motorway corridor and had no Overture buildings. Five larger faces had 13–80 mapped buildings and visually corresponded to residential blocks, including a narrow frontage wedge whose motorway exposure was **0.674**. The prior `exposure ≥ 0.30` deletion rule would erase that real wedge. The 13 small slivers are strong review candidates, but the original sketches for three southern blocks stopped before their enclosing street. The raw 3/5 score therefore is **not** a valid accuracy estimate; see the [post-freeze adjudication](motorway_reference_adjudication_v1.json). Two wedge sketches remain provisional pending public-road status review. No numeric precision or recall is claimed from this core.
6. The [building-support audit](motorway_building_support.csv) reproduced the 18 motorway-core candidates and 35 earlier stress candidates. Buildings help identify the narrow residential wedge, but are not a mandatory criterion for blockhood: parks and vacant blocks have no buildings, and several earlier 100%-motorway-exposed faces contained mapped buildings. Automatic rejection using building presence or a single motorway-buffer threshold is unsupported.
7. A [named-street gap screen](named_street_gap_screen_summary.json) examined 3,183 eligible named municipal segments across five cached Community Areas. With 5 m alignment tolerance and a ≥30 m uncovered run trigger, it found 11 segment alerts on nine names. West Veterans Place has a 71.1 m uncovered run on a 98.0 m segment, reproducing a known merge. Other alerts include lower-level, dead-end and alignment cases; alerts require grade/status and property-side edge checks. They are not an automatic centerline-buffer instruction.
8. M4 remains more uncertain than M3. Simplifying matched ROW outlines by 1–5 m slightly improves compactness agreement with rough rectangular sketches, but the sketches lack surveyed curbs and detailed shape. No simplification distance or M4 distribution can be accepted on this evidence.

## Source and algorithm decision

The strongest current **ordinary-street candidate** is land faces from Cook road ROW (`ROWTYPE ∈ {1,4,5}`) plus ordinary Cook Road Edge (`TYPE=1`), after reopening mapped alley edge (`TYPE=5`) by 3 m. It should be retained as *candidate version*, alongside municipal-centerline `momepy.enclosures` as the established baseline. The 3 m setting is a small-zone development choice and may not transfer to all alley widths or the whole city. The candidate needs a named-street gap-alert and boundary-repair layer. Motorway proximity and building support are reason-coded review fields, not deletion rules. Whole-block geometry must be generated before Community Area overlay and M4 shape calculation.

## What blocks acceptance

- **Independent inventory:** Large, fully enclosed references for ordinary, industrial/private-access, downtown/grade-separated, rail, water, motorway, parks, airport, and DuPage-edge contexts. Both frozen-reference corrections above show why a second reviewer and road-status/plat checks matter. Keep the original hashes and record adjudications separately.
- **Boundary repair:** Determine landward edges for named streets missing from ROW/Road Edge, beginning with West Veterans Place. Until confirmed, preserve `merge_unresolved` rather than inventing a corridor width.
- **Non-block adjudication:** Complete inventory of all faces in motorway zones, including true narrow frontage blocks and empty parks. Evaluate morphology, source boundary, land cover, roads, buildings and parcels jointly, with unresolved outcomes where evidence conflicts. Validate any automatic rejection against fresh unseen zones for false-removal risk.
- **Citywide reliability:** Freeze a revised rule, test it on independent complete zones by context, then process 77 Community Areas with halo stitching, area conservation, topology, source-coverage and uncertainty reports. Establish precision, recall, boundary, area and compactness thresholds against reviewer disagreement and Chicago model sensitivity before producing M3/M4.
