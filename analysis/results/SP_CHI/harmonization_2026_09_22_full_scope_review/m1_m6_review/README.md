# M1/M6 matched-road scope review

This checkpoint audits the sealed H1–H3 matched Overture road candidates without changing their numeric files or fitting a model. Run `analysis/scripts/review_m1_m6_scope_v1.py` for the full 173-unit comparison, `review_m1_geometry_alignment_v1.py` for sampled paired linework checks, `review_m1_outlier_classes_v1.py` and `review_m1_outlier_nearest_class_v1.py` for the two largest source-universe outliers, and `review_pedestrian_street_boundaries_v1.py` for the block-network consistency check. `render_m1_outlier_maps_v1.py` reproduces the [O'Hare](Chicago_76_m1_outlier.png) and [Marsilac](SP_52_m1_outlier.png) maps. The 15 m linework comparison is length-weighted using points spaced at most 20 m apart; it is an approximate positional diagnostic, not an exact line union or acceptance cutoff. The synthetic sampling fixture passes.

The shared M1 candidate measures length of ten selected Overture street classes per gross reporting area, retaining carriageways and links, excluding service/alleys/driveways, tracks, footways and nonstreet paths, and including designated pedestrian streets. Boundary-clipped length is assigned once; the source run conserved citywide assigned length within 1 m. It describes **mapped streets regardless of legal access**; absent access restrictions are not evidence of public access. M6 is the ten length shares of that same network, with no imputation. All 173 class-share rows sum to one and all M1 values reconstruct from numerator/denominator.

The local networks use different construction and class policies; these correlations do not prove complete physical-street coverage. At 15 m, four urban pilots (CHI:24/28/30/32) have 94.0–98.3% of eligible Overture length near local lines, and Brás/SP:35 have 97.1–97.2%. Edge cases differ: CHI:76 has only 72.4% of Overture length near local and **41.7% of local length near Overture**; SP:52 has 88.0%/54.6%. Tolerance results at 5/15/30 m and the other pilots are in `paired_linework_alignment.csv`.

O'Hare's city-local network contains **141.2 km of class 99**, which the city's own metadata labels “Unclassified (O'Hare).” Only about 20.7% is within 15 m of the *eligible* Overture street network. The nearest raw Overture road class to 30.6 km is `service`; **83.0 km has no raw Overture road within 15 m**. The local map shows much of code 99 inside the airfield, so those lines cannot be silently treated as ordinary streets. In rural Marsilac (SP:52), local `LOCAL` lines total 209.8 km; 66.2 km lie near Overture `unclassified`, 40.2 km near excluded `track`, and 45.4 km have no raw Overture road within 15 m. A further 85.8 km of municipal lines have unresolved local class, including 20.2 km near `track` and 21.2 km beyond 15 m from any raw Overture road. These are mixed policy and mapping-coverage gaps, not a single correction factor.

The [Overture RoadClass reference](https://docs.overturemaps.org/schema/reference/transportation/types/segment/road_class/) defines `unclassified` as a **known paved lower-order road**; `unknown` means undetermined class in the [segment/connector guide](https://docs.overturemaps.org/guides/transportation/segments-and-connectors/). Keep them separate. District-level `unknown` reaches 5.3% in Chicago and 7.0% in SP; `unclassified` reaches 22.4% at O'Hare and 54.7% in Marsilac. Do not recode either city’s unknown mass to Local or collapse known `unclassified` into unknown. The Overture [road guide](https://docs.overturemaps.org/guides/transportation/roads/) also leaves implied access to local rules; M1/M6 cannot be described as publicly accessible roads.

**Pilot consistency defect:** the older prepared road layer used by M2/M3/M4 pilots has nine classes and omits `pedestrian`, while H1–H3 M1/M6 has ten. Restoring pedestrian lines in eight no-link block pilots adds at most seven owned enclosures (CHI:28); reference-match changes are small, but future block-network versions must use the frozen ten-class street source or explicitly justify a separate boundary universe. `pedestrian_boundary_sensitivity.csv` preserves the paired effect.

**Decision:** lock the ten-class source and M1 length/M6 composition definitions for continued review; retain both as **coverage-qualified candidates**, not strict accepted family inputs yet. Resolve the Marsilac/rural coverage gap and O'Hare airfield representation in source-completeness sensitivity, then test the influence of those outliers on any eventual SP fit. The remaining 13-family gates still prohibit a shared fit or ranking.
