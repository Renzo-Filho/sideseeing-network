# U1 independent case audit: physical cover and named destinations

**23 September 2026. Small, single-reviewer diagnostic audit.** This follows the [six 400 m OSM tile pilot](../u1_osm_tiles_2026_09_23/README.md). It tests selected *cases*, not district-wide source accuracy. We did not fit or rank the 173-unit model, adopt U1, or assign weight to a POI variable.

## Questions and frozen sample

1. What is **visibly on the ground** at saved São Paulo fiscal-no-lot and Chicago CMAP `6000` points? Does a broad OSM `landuse` polygon offer a valid use label at those points?
2. For a few named OSM/Overture venue records, can evidence **outside those inventories** support existence, approximate placement and category? Can two agreeing inventories share a questionable coordinate or taxonomy?

The land sample was fixed in `audit_u1_land_cases.py` before image review: SP:10 samples 9 and 20, SP:02 sample 2, SP:95 sample 7, and CHI:32/14/23 samples 5/3/10. Six have previously saved source-context labels; SP:10 sample 20 adds a second no-lot/inside-ordinary-block contrast, and SP:95 sample 7 is a no-lot/outside-block contrast. These are **purposive** cases, not probability samples. Coordinates and municipal-street distances are in [land_case_geometry.csv](land_case_geometry.csv). An earlier SP:95 tile was centered on fiscal-residential sample 1; this audit deliberately uses sample 7 to inspect a missing-lot case.

The seven POI cases were frozen by `audit_u1_poi_cases.py` **before external searches**. It selected the minimum SHA-256 of `20260923-u1-case-audit-v1:unit:stratum:source_id` among named records in each requested tile/stratum: Brás matched and OSM-only; Alto de Pinheiros and São Domingos Overture-only; Loop matched, OSM-only and Overture-only. A match still means only exact normalized name within 30 m in local projected coordinates. [Selected records](selected_poi_cases.csv), [eligible pool counts](poi_case_frames.csv) and [selection rule](poi_selection_receipt.json) allow this small sample to be reproduced. The seven cases are not an unbiased accuracy sample of either inventory.

## Reference evidence and adjudication standard

For land, we inspected **small aerial chips** centered exactly on each point, with a red marker, plus municipal street centerline distance. Chicago chips came from the [Cook County imagery service labeled 2025](https://gis.cookcountyil.gov/imagery/rest/services/CookOrtho2025/ImageServer); the [county imagery item](https://hub-cookcountyil.opendata.arcgis.com/datasets/cookcountyil::cook-county-aerial-imagery-2025/about) describes 2025 six-inch aerial photography. São Paulo chips came from [Esri World Imagery](https://server.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer), which is **independent of the fiscal map but has no verified capture date for these pixels**. Physical cover visible in imagery is not a legal parcel, current business activity, or primary land use. City road centerlines can omit alleys and access ways. The image URLs and SHA-256s are in [imagery_receipts.json](imagery_receipts.json); raw and annotated chips remain in the local ignored `analysis/work/u1_independent_cases_2026_09_23/` directory. A cache-fill rerun reproduced every original image SHA-256 exactly. Review was by **one unblinded reviewer** and should be repeated independently before estimating error rates.

For POIs, we separated **venue existence**, **location** and **category**. Sources include operator websites, public business/venue directories, a bounded official [Chicago active-license](https://data.cityofchicago.org/Community-Economic-Development/Business-Licenses-Current-Active/uupf-x98q) record, and municipal São Paulo street-name/house-number ranges. A directory or social search excerpt is weaker than an operator page or official administrative record. A street number range locates a possible segment, **not an exact entrance**; the distances below are conservative bounds from the mapped POI point to a segment that could contain the published number. They do not establish whether a venue moved between source dates. Source URLs and case-level caveats are in [poi_case_labels.csv](poi_case_labels.csv), with the address computations in [sp_poi_address_ranges.csv](sp_poi_address_ranges.csv). A failed exact-string web search is coded **unverified**, never “nonexistent.”

[Case labels and confidence](land_case_labels.csv). The SP imagery's unknown vintage and visual ambiguity in two cases limit temporal and functional conclusions. The clear rail/street cases show that some fiscal no-lot area is **real nonparcel transport space**; they do not tell us the *fraction* of the 7–8.5% no-lot/inside-block district land that has this cause. The Chicago cases show the opposite failure mode: broad OSM polygon coverage can be numerically high while it labels an alley or street as residential. This is a semantic/spatial-support problem, not a proof that OSM is globally inaccurate.

## What the test changes

- **U1 area repair:** the no-lot/inside-block hypothesis was too broad. At least one such point is a rail corridor; one remains ambiguous. Broader OSM `landuse` polygons also visibly cross Chicago nonparcel road/alley support. We should not fill either source residual by overlaying OSM polygons or assigning venue points to parcel area.
- **POI diversity:** a mapped venue variable might still capture useful function, but this small sample exposes *separate* existence, location and category errors. A high Overture confidence value did not protect The Ledge's category; an OSM/Overture name/location match did not independently confirm the mall's published address; absence from OSM did not refute New Heaven. Provider and taxonomy checks must accompany any destination mix. No precision, recall, entropy correction, city comparison or model weight is estimated here.
- **Next study:** have a second reviewer label the seven imagery chips and venue evidence without source labels, resolve disagreements, then draw a **stratified probability sample with saved inclusion probabilities** from no-lot land inside/outside blocks, Chicago `6000`, and POI provider/name/taxonomy strata. Add imagery acquisition date and address-vintage checks. Only then estimate support/error rates and decide if another area source or a separately named destination feature meets the common-model gate.

## Reproduce the bounded checks

```bash
.venv/bin/python analysis/scripts/audit_u1_land_cases.py
.venv/bin/python analysis/scripts/audit_u1_poi_cases.py
.venv/bin/python analysis/scripts/audit_u1_poi_neighbors.py
.venv/bin/python analysis/scripts/audit_u1_poi_address_ranges.py
```

The imagery script makes only seven small chip mosaics/exports, caches the individual bounded source images by request URL, stores image hashes and computes seven municipal-street distances. The POI selection reads only four of the existing six tiles from local OSM XML plus bounded Parquet reads from pinned Overture Places **2026-08-19.0**; the neighbor script reads two bounded tiles. Address checks read one named São Paulo municipal street at a time. All live sources can change; preserve these receipts and case labels as the 23 September observation. `land_case_labels.csv` and `poi_case_labels.csv` are human-review products, not automatic outputs of the scripts. No citywide process or model fit was run.
